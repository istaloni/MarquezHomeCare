Who we are

Built on family values and local trust

At Marquez Family Home Care, we believe everyone deserves to age with dignity, comfort and peace of mind. As a family-owned agency based here in San Diego, we understand how much care goes into arranging help for someone you love.

We don't believe in generic care or constant caregiver turnover. We match every client with a vetted, patient caregiver who treats them with the same warmth we bring to our own family.

Our services

Quick answers

Questions families often ask

Do you provide medical care?

No. We provide non-medical support such as personal care, meals, errands and companionship, and we work alongside your loved one's medical team rather than replacing it.

Will my mom have the same caregiver each time?

That's our goal. We match caregivers for the long term so your loved one sees a familiar face and can build a real relationship with the person helping them.

How do you choose your caregivers?

Every caregiver completes DOJ and FBI fingerprinting, is registered with the California Home Care Aide Registry, has TB clearance and receives ongoing training.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve families throughout San Diego County. If you're not sure whether we reach your neighborhood, give us a call and we'll let you know.
